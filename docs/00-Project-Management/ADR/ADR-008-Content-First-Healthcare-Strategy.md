# ADR-008 — Content-First Healthcare Strategy

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 30 July 2026

**Decision Makers:** Product Team

---

# Context

Patients often seek trustworthy information before scheduling consultations. Content should educate, build confidence, and support informed decision-making rather than simply promote services.

---

# Decision Drivers

The following priorities guided this decision:

- Patient education
- Trust building
- Clear communication
- SEO
- Accessibility
- Long-term content growth

---

# Decision

The website will adopt a content-first strategy.

Every page will prioritize clear, accurate, and patient-friendly information before promotional messaging.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Marketing-first content

### Pros

- Strong promotional focus

### Cons

- Reduced credibility
- Lower educational value

---

## Minimal content

### Pros

- Faster implementation

### Cons

- Poor SEO
- Limited patient understanding

---

# Rationale

Educational content establishes credibility, improves search visibility, and helps prospective patients make informed decisions.

---

# Consequences

## Positive

- Greater patient trust
- Improved SEO
- Better engagement
- Stronger brand authority

## Trade-offs

- Requires ongoing content maintenance
- Greater editorial effort

---

# Impact

## Product Impact

Strengthens the clinic's reputation as a trusted source of orthodontic information.

## Engineering Impact

Requires flexible content structures that support future expansion.

## User Impact

Patients receive useful information before contacting the clinic, improving confidence and reducing uncertainty.
