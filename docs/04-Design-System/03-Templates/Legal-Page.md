# Legal Page Template Specification

**Project:** Dr. Pharande Orthodontic Clinic – Premium Website Redesign

**Template:** Legal Pages

**Owner:** Product Team

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Last Updated:** 31 July 2026

---

# Purpose

The Legal Page template provides a standardized structure for all legal and policy-related documents published by the clinic.

It ensures legal information remains consistent, readable, accessible, and easy to maintain while supporting regulatory compliance.

---

# Supported Documents

This template applies to:

- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions
- Cookie Policy
- Accessibility Statement
- Disclaimer
- Refund Policy (if applicable)

Additional legal documents should reuse this template whenever possible.

---

# Business Objectives

Primary objectives:

- Provide transparent legal information.
- Improve user confidence.
- Support regulatory compliance.

Secondary objectives:

- Maintain consistency.
- Simplify maintenance.
- Improve readability.
- Reduce legal ambiguity.

---

# User Goals

Visitors should be able to:

- Understand their rights.
- Understand clinic policies.
- Find important legal information quickly.
- Contact the clinic for clarification if necessary.

---

# Target Audience

Primary:

- Prospective patients.
- Existing patients.

Secondary:

- Parents.
- Legal representatives.
- Regulatory authorities.

---

# Components Used

The Legal Page assembles:

- Navigation
- Typography
- Cards (Optional)
- CTA (Optional)
- Footer

No promotional components should interrupt legal content.

---

# Information Architecture

```
Navigation

↓

Document Title

↓

Last Updated

↓

Introduction

↓

Policy Sections

↓

Contact Information

↓

Footer
```

---

# Page Header

Display:

- Document title.
- Short description.
- Last updated date.

Example:

```
Privacy Policy

Last Updated:
31 July 2026
```

---

# Introduction

Briefly explain:

- Purpose of the document.
- Scope.
- Intended audience.

Avoid legal jargon where plain language is sufficient.

---

# Policy Sections

Use clear section hierarchy.

Example:

```
1. Information We Collect

↓

2. How We Use Information

↓

3. Cookies

↓

4. Data Security

↓

5. Your Rights

↓

6. Contact Information
```

Use numbered headings for easier navigation.

---

# Content Guidelines

Legal content should:

- Be accurate.
- Be concise.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Use plain language where possible.
- Be reviewed before publication.

---

# Contact Information

Provide:

- Clinic name.
- Email.
- Phone.
- Postal address.

Users should know how to ask questions regarding the document.

---

# Navigation

The global website navigation should remain available.

However, legal pages should minimize distractions.

No promotional banners should appear between policy sections.

---

# Optional CTA

A CTA may appear after the legal content.

Examples:

```
Contact Our Team

Book a Consultation
```

The CTA should remain visually secondary to the legal information.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

## Mobile

- Single-column layout.
- Comfortable reading width.
- Clear spacing between sections.

---

## Tablet

- Improved whitespace.
- Better typography scaling.

---

## Desktop

- Maximum readable line length.
- Wide margins.
- Comfortable reading experience.

---

# Accessibility

The template must support:

- Semantic HTML.
- Proper heading hierarchy.
- Keyboard navigation.
- Screen readers.
- Skip links.
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliance.

Legal content should remain understandable when zoomed to 200%.

---

# Performance

Performance targets:

- LCP < 2.5 seconds
- CLS < 0.1
- INP < 200 ms

Recommendations:

- Minimal JavaScript.
- Lightweight layout.
- No unnecessary media.

---

# SEO Considerations

Each legal document should include:

- Unique title tag.
- Meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Structured heading hierarchy.
- Internal links where appropriate.

Legal pages should generally not compete with marketing pages for search rankings.

---

# Analytics

Recommended events:

```
Legal Page Viewed

Scroll Depth

```

Analytics should respect applicable privacy regulations.
