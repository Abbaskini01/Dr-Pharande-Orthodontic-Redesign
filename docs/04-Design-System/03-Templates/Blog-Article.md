# Blog Article Template Specification

**Project:** Dr. Pharande Orthodontic Clinic – Premium Website Redesign

**Template:** Blog Article

**Owner:** Product Team

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Last Updated:** 31 July 2026

---

# Purpose

The Blog Article template provides a standardized structure for publishing educational content that informs patients, strengthens the clinic's credibility, improves search engine visibility, and encourages consultation requests.

Every article should prioritize education before conversion.

---

# Business Objectives

Primary objectives:

- Increase organic search traffic.
- Educate prospective patients.
- Generate consultation enquiries.

Secondary objectives:

- Increase engagement.
- Build authority.
- Improve internal linking.
- Encourage repeat visitors.

---

# User Goals

Readers should be able to:

- Understand the topic quickly.
- Navigate long-form content easily.
- Access trustworthy information.
- Explore related treatments.
- Book a consultation when appropriate.

---

# Target Audience

Primary:

- Prospective orthodontic patients.
- Parents researching treatment options.

Secondary:

- Existing patients.
- Referring dentists.
- Healthcare professionals.

---

# Components Used

The template assembles the following approved components:

- Navigation
- Hero
- Typography
- Cards
- CTA
- Accordion (Optional)
- Footer

---

# Article Hero

## Purpose

Introduce the article and communicate its topic clearly.

Include:

- Article title
- Featured image
- Category
- Reading time
- Publication date

---

# Author Information

Display:

- Author name
- Professional title
- Credentials
- Profile photograph (Optional)

This section reinforces trust and expertise.

---

# Article Metadata

Recommended metadata:

- Published date
- Last updated date
- Reading time
- Category
- Tags (Optional)

---

# Table of Contents

Required for long-form articles.

Should:

- Link to headings.
- Collapse on mobile.
- Remain sticky on desktop where appropriate.

---

# Article Content

Recommended hierarchy:

```
Introduction

↓

Problem

↓

Explanation

↓

Treatment Options

↓

Frequently Asked Questions

↓

Summary

↓

Consultation CTA
```

Content should use short paragraphs and meaningful headings.

---

# Images & Educational Graphics

Media may include:

- Clinical diagrams
- Orthodontic illustrations
- Treatment photographs
- Educational infographics

Requirements:

- Optimized images.
- Descriptive alternative text.
- Responsive display.

---

# Related Articles

Display three to four relevant articles.

Example:

```
Ceramic Braces

↓

Invisalign

↓

Retainers

↓

Orthodontic Consultation
```

---

# Consultation CTA

Conclude the article with a clear call-to-action.

Examples:

```
Book Your Consultation

Talk to Our Orthodontist

Schedule an Appointment
```

Only one primary CTA should receive visual emphasis.

---

# Content Guidelines

Content should:

- Use plain language.
- Be medically accurate.
- Explain technical terminology.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Reference evidence where appropriate.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

## Mobile

- Single-column layout.
- Full-width media.
- Collapsible table of contents.

---

## Tablet

- Flexible spacing.
- Optimized images.

---

## Desktop

- Comfortable reading width.
- Sticky table of contents (Optional).
- Balanced whitespace.

---

# Accessibility

The template must support:

- Semantic HTML landmarks.
- Keyboard navigation.
- Logical heading hierarchy.
- Screen readers.
- Accessible images.
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliance.

---

# Performance

Performance targets:

- LCP < 2.5 seconds
- CLS < 0.1
- INP < 200 ms

Recommendations:

- Optimize featured images.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold media.
- Compress assets.
- Minimize render-blocking resources.

---

# SEO Considerations

Every article should include:

- Unique title tag.
- Unique meta description.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph metadata.
- Article schema.
- Breadcrumb schema.
- Internal links.
- Optimized images.
