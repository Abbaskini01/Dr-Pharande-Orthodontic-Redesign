# About Page Template Specification

**Project:** Dr. Pharande Orthodontic Clinic – Premium Website Redesign

**Template:** About Page

**Owner:** Product Team

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Last Updated:** 31 July 2026

---

# Purpose

The About Page introduces the clinic, orthodontist, and team while building trust through authentic storytelling, professional credentials, and a patient-centered philosophy.

The page should help visitors feel confident about choosing the clinic before scheduling a consultation.

---

# Business Objectives

Primary objectives:

- Build credibility.
- Increase consultation bookings.
- Strengthen brand trust.

Secondary objectives:

- Differentiate the clinic.
- Highlight expertise.
- Showcase modern facilities.
- Reinforce patient confidence.

---

# User Goals

Visitors should quickly understand:

- Who the orthodontist is.
- Why the clinic is trustworthy.
- The clinic's philosophy.
- The technology and facilities available.
- Why they should choose this clinic.

---

# Target Audience

Primary:

- Prospective patients.
- Parents researching orthodontic care.

Secondary:

- Existing patients.
- Referring dentists.
- Healthcare professionals.

---

# Components Used

The About Page assembles:

- Navigation
- Hero
- Cards
- Gallery
- CTA
- Testimonials
- Footer

# Hero Section

## Purpose

Introduce the clinic and establish a welcoming first impression.

Include:

- Page title.
- Supporting description.
- Professional hero image.
- Primary CTA.

Example:

```
Meet the Team Behind Confident Smiles

Dedicated Orthodontic Care for Every Stage of Life.
```

---

# Doctor Profile

Include:

- Professional portrait.
- Qualifications.
- Years of experience.
- Professional memberships.
- Areas of expertise.
- Short biography.

Content should focus on experience and patient care rather than self-promotion.

---

# Clinic Story

Describe:

- When the clinic was established.
- Growth over time.
- Commitment to patient care.
- Community involvement (if applicable).

The story should feel authentic and concise.

---

# Mission & Values

Clearly communicate guiding principles.

Example values:

- Compassion
- Clinical Excellence
- Integrity
- Innovation
- Patient-Centered Care

Use cards or icon-supported content for readability.

---

# Technology & Facilities

Highlight modern capabilities such as:

- Digital X-rays
- 3D Imaging
- Intraoral Scanners
- Sterilization Standards
- Comfortable Treatment Rooms

Focus on how technology benefits patients.

---

# Meet the Team (Optional)

Display key team members.

Each profile may include:

- Name
- Role
- Photograph
- Short description

Avoid excessive detail.

---

# Achievements & Certifications

Display:

- Professional certifications.
- Awards.
- Memberships.
- Continuing education.
- Recognitions.

Present information visually using cards where appropriate.

---

# Clinic Gallery

Include photographs of:

- Reception area.
- Treatment rooms.
- Equipment.
- Waiting area.
- Team interactions.

Requirements:

- High-resolution images.
- Consistent visual style.
- Patient privacy maintained.

---

# Patient Testimonials

Include a curated selection of authentic patient feedback.

Each testimonial should contain:

- Patient name (where permitted).
- Treatment type (optional).
- Quote.

Limit the number displayed to maintain focus.

---

# Consultation CTA

Conclude the page with a clear invitation.

Examples:

```
Book Your Consultation

Meet Our Orthodontist

Start Your Smile Journey
```

Only one primary CTA should receive visual emphasis.

---

# Content Guidelines

Content should:

- Be authentic.
- Be patient-focused.
- Use approachable language.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Maintain a warm and professional tone.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

## Mobile

- Single-column layout.
- Full-width images.
- Stacked cards.

---

## Tablet

- Flexible spacing.
- Two-column layouts where appropriate.

---

## Desktop

- Multi-column composition.
- Larger imagery.
- Comfortable whitespace.

---

# Accessibility

The About Page must support:

- Semantic landmarks.
- Keyboard navigation.
- Screen readers.
- Accessible images.
- Logical heading hierarchy.
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliance.

---

# Performance

Performance targets:

- LCP < 2.5 seconds
- CLS < 0.1
- INP < 200 ms

Recommendations:

- Optimize photography.
- Lazy-load gallery images.
- Compress media assets.
- Minimize render-blocking resources.

---

# SEO Considerations

The About Page should include:

- Unique title tag.
- Unique meta description.
- Organization schema.
- Person schema for the orthodontist.
- Canonical URL.
- Optimized image alt text.
- Internal links to treatments and contact page.
