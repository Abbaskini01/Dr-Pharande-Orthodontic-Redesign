# Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope

**Project:** Dr. Pharande Orthodontic Clinic – Premium Website Redesign

**Document Owner:** Product Team

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Last Updated:** 30 July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the minimum feature set required for Version 1 (MVP) of the redesigned website.

The MVP should deliver a complete, high-quality patient experience while avoiding unnecessary complexity.

---

# 2. MVP Objective

Launch a production-ready website that:

- Builds trust
- Educates patients
- Improves appointment conversion
- Represents the clinic's premium brand
- Performs exceptionally on all devices

Version 1 focuses on solving the most important patient and business problems first.

---

# 3. Product Goals

The MVP should enable visitors to:

- Learn about the clinic
- Understand available treatments
- Build confidence in the doctor
- Book an appointment easily
- Contact the clinic without friction

---

# 4. In-Scope Features

## Homepage

### Purpose

Create a strong first impression and guide users toward relevant information.

### Includes

- Hero section
- Value proposition
- Doctor introduction
- Featured treatments
- Testimonials preview
- Trust indicators
- Primary CTA
- Footer

---

## About the Doctor

### Includes

- Professional biography
- Qualifications
- Experience
- Certifications
- Philosophy of care
- Professional photography

---

## Treatments

Create dedicated pages for each major treatment.

Each page should include:

- Overview
- Benefits
- Treatment process
- FAQs
- Expected duration
- Suitable candidates
- Call to Action

---

## Before & After Gallery

Purpose:

Build credibility through real treatment outcomes.

Requirements:

- Responsive gallery
- High-quality images
- Organized categories
- Optimized loading

---

## Testimonials

Display authentic patient reviews.

Include:

- Patient name (where appropriate)
- Treatment type
- Rating
- Optional photo

---

## FAQ

Answer common patient questions.

Examples:

- Treatment duration
- Cost expectations
- Pain management
- Consultation process
- Payment options

---

## Appointment Booking

Requirements:

- Simple form
- Mobile optimized
- Clear confirmation
- Contact alternatives
- Validation
- Success state

---

## Contact Page

Include:

- Address
- Google Maps
- Phone
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Clinic hours
- Contact form

---

## Blog

Educational content hub.

Initial categories:

- Braces
- Invisalign
- Oral Hygiene
- Adult Orthodontics
- Parents Guide

---

# 5. Global Features

The entire website must support:

- Responsive layout
- Fast loading
- Accessibility
- SEO optimization
- Consistent branding
- Analytics integration

---

# 6. Technical Requirements

Version 1 will include:

- Next.js
- React
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion
- Vercel Deployment

---

# 7. Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from Version 1.

## AI Features

- AI Chat Assistant
- Smile Assessment AI
- AI Appointment Assistant

---

## Patient Platform

- Login
- Dashboard
- Appointment History
- Treatment Progress

---

## Payments

- Online Payment Gateway
- EMI Calculator
- Invoice Portal

---

## Administration

- Doctor Dashboard
- Appointment Management
- CMS

---

## Advanced Integrations

- CRM
- Marketing Automation
- SMS Notifications
- Email Campaigns

---

# 8. MVP Definition of Done

Version 1 is complete when:

- All planned pages are implemented.
- Responsive testing is complete.
- Lighthouse scores exceed targets.
- Accessibility requirements are met.
- SEO implementation is complete.
- Booking workflow functions correctly.
- Content is reviewed.
- Production deployment succeeds.

---

# 9. Success Criteria

The MVP successfully launches if it:

- Represents the clinic professionally.
- Delivers a smooth patient journey.
- Improves usability over the previous website.
- Meets engineering quality standards.
- Is ready for future expansion.
