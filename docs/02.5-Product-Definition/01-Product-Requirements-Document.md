# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Project:** Dr. Pharande Orthodontic Clinic – Premium Website Redesign

**Document Owner:** Product Team

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Last Updated:** 30 July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the functional vision, business objectives, product scope, and implementation requirements for the redesign of Dr. Pharande Orthodontic Clinic's website.

It serves as the primary reference for product managers, designers, developers, and future contributors throughout the project lifecycle.

---

# 2. Background

Research conducted during the Discovery and Strategy phases identified opportunities to significantly improve the clinic's digital presence.

Current challenges include:

- Outdated visual identity
- Weak differentiation from competitors
- Limited patient education
- Suboptimal appointment booking experience
- Lack of modern product thinking
- Missed SEO opportunities

The redesign aims to address these issues through a user-centered, scalable, and performance-focused product.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Patients researching orthodontic treatment often experience uncertainty before scheduling a consultation.

The current website does not sufficiently:

- Build trust
- Educate patients
- Simplify decision-making
- Guide users toward booking
- Reflect the premium quality of the clinic

---

# 4. Product Vision

Deliver a premium digital patient experience that increases confidence, improves accessibility, and converts interested visitors into qualified consultation requests.

---

# 5. Business Goals

The redesigned website should:

- Increase consultation bookings.
- Improve local brand perception.
- Strengthen search visibility.
- Educate prospective patients.
- Establish a scalable digital foundation for future enhancements.

---

# 6. Success Metrics

The project targets:

- Lighthouse Performance ≥ 95
- Accessibility ≥ 95
- SEO ≥ 95
- Best Practices ≥ 95
- Excellent mobile usability
- Faster appointment journey
- Improved engagement with treatment pages

Detailed measurement criteria are documented in:

`02-Strategy/06-Success-Metrics.md`

---

# 7. Target Users

## Primary Users

- Teenagers seeking orthodontic treatment
- Parents researching treatment for children
- Working professionals
- Adults seeking smile correction

---

## Secondary Users

- Existing patients
- Referral dentists
- Family members

---

# 8. User Needs

Patients need to:

- Understand available treatments.
- Learn about the doctor.
- Estimate treatment expectations.
- Build confidence.
- Book appointments easily.
- Contact the clinic quickly.

---

# 9. Product Scope

## In Scope

- Homepage
- About Doctor
- Treatment Pages
- Appointment Booking
- Contact
- FAQ
- Testimonials
- Before & After Gallery
- Educational Blog
- Responsive Design
- SEO
- Accessibility
- Performance Optimization

---

## Out of Scope (Version 1)

- Online Payments
- Patient Dashboard
- Doctor Portal
- AI Chat Assistant
- Multi-language Support
- Appointment Management System

These items are reserved for future releases.

---

# 10. Constraints

The project should:

- Be mobile-first.
- Meet WCAG AA accessibility standards.
- Maintain high performance.
- Use reusable components.
- Follow documented design principles.
- Be easily maintainable.

---

# 11. Assumptions

The project assumes:

- Existing content can be refined.
- Professional photography will be available.
- Appointment booking workflow remains unchanged.
- Future AI features may be introduced without architectural redesign.
