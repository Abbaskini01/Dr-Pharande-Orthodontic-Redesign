# Overview

The Research section documents the evidence used to make user experience decisions throughout the project.

Rather than relying on assumptions, design decisions should be supported by research gathered from users, stakeholders, competitors, existing products, analytics, and established UX best practices.

Research provides the foundation for every subsequent stage of the design process.

---

# Objectives

This section aims to:

- Understand user needs.
- Identify business goals.
- Validate design decisions.
- Reduce usability risks.
- Improve accessibility.
- Support evidence-based product development.
- Create a shared understanding across the team.

---

# Research Principles

Every research activity should be:

- Ethical
- Objective
- Reproducible
- Well documented
- Actionable

Avoid unsupported assumptions whenever possible.

---

# Documentation Standards

Each research document should include:

- Purpose
- Scope
- Methodology
- Findings
- Insights
- Recommendations
- References
- Revision History

---

# Validation

Research findings should answer at least one of the following:

- What problem exists?
- Why does the problem exist?
- Who is affected?
- How severe is the problem?
- What evidence supports the finding?
- What recommendation follows?

---

# Research Quality Criteria

Good research should be:

- Relevant
- Accurate
- Current
- Evidence-based
- Easy to understand

# Success Criteria

The Research phase is complete when:

- Target users are documented.
- User journeys are validated.
- Competitor analysis is completed.
- UX goals are defined.
- Findings are referenced by later design documents.
